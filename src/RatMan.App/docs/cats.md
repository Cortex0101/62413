# Ratman Cats
## Blinky (red cat)
Follows Ratman directly during Chase mode, and heads to the upper-right corner during Scatter mode.

## Inky (pink cat)
During Chase mode, his target is a bit complex. His target is relative to both Blinky and Ratman, where the distance Blinky is from Pinky's target is doubled to get Inky's target. He heads to the lower-right corner during Scatter mode.    

## Pinky (cyan cat)
Chases towards the spot 2 Pac-Dots in front of Ratman. Due to a bug in the original game's coding, if Ratman faces upwards, Pinky's target will be 2 tiles in front of and 2 to the left of Ratman. During Scatter mode, she heads towards the upper-left corner.

## Clyde (Orange cat)
Chases directly after Ratman, but tries to head to his Scatter corner when within an 8-Dot radius of Ratman. His Scatter Mode corner is the lower-left.
